# Tip Sheet 2 — Support

Thanks for using Tip Sheet 2! If you need help, have a question, or want to share feedback, we're happy to hear from you.

## Contact

- **In the app:** Go to **Settings → Contact Support**. You'll be asked whether to **Attach Report** (which includes non-financial app diagnostics such as iCloud sync status and recent crash logs to help us troubleshoot faster) or **Don't Attach**.
- **Email:** **support@tipsheetapp.com**

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add a shift?
Tap the **+** button on the main screen to log a new shift. Enter your start/end time, tips, and any notes, then save. You can configure default jobs, start times, and location-based job selection under **Settings → Shift Defaults**.

### How do I import data from the original Tip Sheet (v1)?
Go to **Settings → Import Tip Sheet Backup** and select either a `.tsjson` backup file or a `.csv` data export from the original Tip Sheet app. Imported shifts are preserved in your history and **do not count toward the 15-shift free tier limit**.

### How does iCloud sync work?
If you're signed into iCloud, Tip Sheet 2 automatically syncs your shifts, jobs, and preferences across all of your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit and iCloud Key-Value Storage.

You can check your sync health anytime under **Settings → iCloud**:
- **On** (with a **Last synced** timestamp) — your data is backed up and syncing normally.
- **Syncing…** — an import or export is currently in progress.
- **Off** — your device isn't signed into iCloud. Tap **Open Settings** to sign in.
- **Restricted / Unavailable** — iCloud is restricted on the device or temporarily unreachable; your shifts are still saved safely on-device.

### Can I track multiple jobs and positions?
Yes. Go to **Jobs** to add as many jobs as you need. Each job can have its own positions, effective-dated hourly rates, daily or weekly overtime rules, pay schedule, and tip-out rules.

### How do I track tips received from coworkers?
To log tips you receive from coworkers (in addition to tips you pay out), enable **Tips from Others** under **Settings → Visible Shift Fields** (it is off by default), or customize visible fields per job.

### How do Reports and Year-over-Year comparisons work?
Open the **Reports** tab to view interactive charts by Day, Week, or Month:
- **Recent** shows rolling time windows that include today's shifts.
- **Year over Year** lets you compare the same week or month across different years.

### How do I export my data?
Go to **Settings → Export Data** to generate a CSV of your shifts, which you can open in Numbers, Excel, or any spreadsheet app.

### What does Tip Sheet 2 Pro unlock?
Tip Sheet 2 Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the free tier's 15-shift limit. It's a lifetime unlock — no subscription. (Shifts imported from Tip Sheet v1 do not count toward the 15-shift limit.)

### I purchased Pro but it's not showing up.
Open **Settings → Restore Purchase**. If the issue persists, contact us via **Settings → Contact Support** or email **support@tipsheetapp.com** with your Apple ID email and we'll help sort it out.

### Where is my data stored?
All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If iCloud is enabled, it also syncs to your personal iCloud account. The developer never has access to your financial or shift data.

### How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes all local data. To also remove iCloud-synced data, go to iOS **Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Tip Sheet 2** and delete the data there.
